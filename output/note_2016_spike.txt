6. Footnote text (methods appendix):
----------------------------------------------------------------------
The apparent surge in measured migration in the 2015-16 and 2016-17
IRS vintages, and the sharp drop from 2018-19 onward, reflect documented
changes in SOI's return-matching methodology rather than population
behavior: gross migrant returns rise roughly a third in each of 2015
and 2016 and fall by half by 2018 while non-migrant counts are stable,
and SOI's own benchmarking (Pierce 2022) shows the IRS mover rate
diverging from ACS and CPS in exactly these vintages before re-aligning.
All specifications include year fixed effects, which absorb these
common shifts; descriptive level comparisons across the 2014-2018
window are avoided, and Hawaii's series are interpreted relative to
the national rate.
----------------------------------------------------------------------
